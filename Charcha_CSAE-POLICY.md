# Charcha App - Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Charcha strictly prohibits any content that exploits or endangers children. We are committed to protecting minors and complying with all applicable child safety laws.

## We do not allow:
- Content that depicts, promotes, or solicits child sexual abuse or exploitation.
- User behavior that targets or harms children.
- Sharing or distributing exploitative or harmful material.

## Enforcement:
We promptly remove any such content and may report violations to appropriate authorities such as the NCMEC and local law enforcement agencies.

If you believe someone is violating these policies, please report it immediately to: **vyvharsplitshare@gmail.com**
